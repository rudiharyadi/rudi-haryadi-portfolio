My Review Workflow

My 10-step approach to accurate and consistent AI evaluation.

01

Read the rubric

Study the project instructions before reviewing the task so every decision follows the required standard.

  • Identify the task objective, scope, and required output format.
  • Understand rating labels, acceptance criteria, and quality thresholds.
  • Review examples, edge cases, prohibited assumptions, and escalation rules.
  • Confirm how unclear, corrupted, unsafe, or insufficient data should be handled.
02

Understand the user intent

Determine what the prompt is truly asking and which requirements must be satisfied in the final response.

  • Separate explicit instructions from reasonable implicit expectations.
  • Identify constraints involving language, tone, length, format, or audience.
  • Determine the main question and avoid being distracted by irrelevant details.
  • Note any conflicting or ambiguous instructions that require escalation.
03

Inspect the source material

Review every available image, prompt, document, reference answer, and model response before making a judgment.

  • Check whether all required materials are available and readable.
  • Review visible text, objects, labels, numbers, and relationships carefully.
  • Identify cropped, blurred, corrupted, duplicated, or incomplete content.
  • Confirm that the source material is relevant to the question.
04

Collect relevant evidence

Extract only the facts needed to answer the task and keep observations separate from assumptions.

  • Record names, dates, values, locations, actions, and visible attributes.
  • Match each conclusion to specific evidence in the source material.
  • Ignore information that does not affect the requested judgment.
  • Avoid guessing when evidence is missing or uncertain.
05

Check factual accuracy

Verify whether every important claim is supported, correct, and consistent with the available evidence.

  • Look for incorrect names, dates, numbers, descriptions, or relationships.
  • Detect unsupported claims, fabricated details, and misleading statements.
  • Compare the answer against references when references are provided.
  • Distinguish a minor imprecision from a material factual error.
06

Evaluate instruction compliance

Confirm that the response follows all explicit and implicit requirements in the prompt and project guideline.

  • Verify requested format, sentence count, language, tone, and structure.
  • Check whether all parts of a multi-part request were completed.
  • Identify unnecessary content that reduces relevance or clarity.
  • Apply the correct label when only part of the instruction was followed.
07

Assess quality and consistency

Review the response for clarity, completeness, coherence, usefulness, and consistency with similar tasks.

  • Check that the answer is understandable to the intended reader.
  • Identify contradictions, repetition, missing steps, or unclear wording.
  • Apply the same decision standard across the entire task batch.
  • Compare borderline cases with approved examples in the rubric.
08

Produce the answer

Create a clear, concise, and evidence-based result using the exact output format required by the project.

  • Answer only what the task asks and remain relevant to the evidence.
  • Use accurate terminology and simple, professional language.
  • Apply the correct rating, label, explanation, or golden-answer format.
  • Keep the explanation specific enough to support the decision.
09

Flag and escalate issues

Handle tasks responsibly when the evidence, instructions, or source material cannot support a reliable decision.

  • Flag unclear, corrupted, unsafe, contradictory, or insufficient data.
  • Explain the reason for escalation using objective observations.
  • Avoid forcing a rating when the rubric requires an exception.
  • Protect confidential or sensitive information during documentation.
10

Run the final QA check

Perform a complete review before submission to reduce avoidable errors and maintain dependable quality.

  • Recheck factual accuracy, completeness, relevance, and compliance.
  • Verify spelling, grammar, formatting, labels, and required fields.
  • Confirm that no unsupported assumption or confidential detail was added.
  • Ensure the final decision is consistent, traceable, and audit-ready.
