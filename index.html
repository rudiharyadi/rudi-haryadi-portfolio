Professional Profile

Reliable technical support meets human-centered AI quality.

My background in IT support has trained me to investigate problems carefully, identify root causes, follow procedures, document evidence, and communicate solutions clearly. During 16 years of practical support work, I learned to begin with the user’s symptoms, collect relevant information, reproduce the issue when possible, check hardware, software, account, and network conditions, and select the safest corrective action. I verify the result after every repair and record what was found, what was changed, and how the incident was resolved.

This structured troubleshooting mindset is highly relevant to AI training and evaluation. Both types of work require close attention to instructions, evidence-based decisions, consistency across repeated tasks, and the discipline to avoid unsupported assumptions. I approach each AI task as a review process: understand the rubric, inspect all available evidence, compare the expected and actual output, classify the issue accurately, and explain the decision in a clear and auditable way.

I am comfortable reviewing images, screenshots, prompts, reference answers, and model responses. I check factual accuracy, relevance, completeness, grammar, formatting, safety, and instruction compliance. I can identify contradictions, hallucinated details, missing information, incorrect labels, visual inconsistencies, and answers that do not match the user’s intent or required output format.

When an image is blurred, cropped, corrupted, or insufficient to answer the question, I do not guess. I document the limitation and flag the task according to the project guideline. When instructions are ambiguous, I review examples and escalation rules before making a decision. This helps maintain consistent quality and reduces avoidable errors across large task batches.

My user-support experience also brings a human-centered perspective to AI quality. A response should not only be technically correct; it should be understandable, relevant, respectful, and useful to the person receiving it. I therefore evaluate both technical correctness and practical clarity, while documenting decisions so reviewers, quality-control specialists, and project managers can follow the reasoning.

This combination of reliable technical support, root-cause analysis, procedural discipline, patient communication, and detailed documentation enables me to contribute effectively as an IT Support Specialist, AI Evaluator, AI Trainer, Data Annotator, Technical Reviewer, or Quality Control Specialist.

My Review Workflow

How I approach an AI evaluation task.

01

Read the rubric

Study the project instructions before reviewing the task so every decision follows the required standard.

  • Identify the task objective, scope, and required output format.
  • Understand rating labels, acceptance criteria, and quality thresholds.
  • Review examples, edge cases, prohibited assumptions, and escalation rules.
  • Confirm how unclear, corrupted, unsafe, or insufficient data should be handled.
02

Inspect evidence

Examine all available visual and textual evidence carefully without adding information that is not supported.

  • Compare the prompt, image, reference material, and model response.
  • Check names, numbers, objects, labels, locations, relationships, and visible details.
  • Look for contradictions, omissions, ambiguity, or insufficient evidence.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions and personal interpretation.
03

Produce answer

Create a clear, concise, and evidence-based answer that follows the requested format exactly.

  • Answer only what the task asks and remain relevant to the evidence.
  • Use accurate terminology and simple language that is easy to understand.
  • Apply the correct rating, label, explanation, or golden-answer format.
  • Flag the task when the evidence is unclear or cannot support a reliable answer.
04

Run QA check

Perform a final quality review before submission to reduce avoidable errors and maintain consistency.

  • Recheck factual accuracy, completeness, relevance, and instruction compliance.
  • Verify spelling, grammar, formatting, labels, and required fields.
  • Confirm that no unsupported claim or confidential information was added.
  • Ensure the final answer is consistent with the rubric and ready for audit.
